Changes made for this app: only the verb senses of each infinitive in the app's verb list are kept;
usage labels (transitive, figurative, ...) come from each sense's qualifiers; at most three short
example sentences are kept per sense, with their English translations; long or untranslated
quotations are left out; for 11 reflexive verbs with no reflexive sense on Wiktionary, the base
verb's meanings are shown. The wording of meanings and examples is unchanged.
