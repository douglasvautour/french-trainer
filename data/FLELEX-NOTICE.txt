FLELex gives, for each word, how often it appears in French textbooks and readers at each CEFR level.
Changes made for this app: each verb and noun in the app gets one level, the first level at which
FLELex records it at least once per million words (a word never that frequent takes the level where
it is most frequent). Words FLELex doesn't list have no level. Only these levels are kept, not the
frequencies.

levels.js is adapted from FLELex and is shared under the same licence, CC BY-NC 4.0: it may not be
used commercially. The levels shown for tenses, pronouns and determiners are not from FLELex; they
follow the usual order of French courses and are part of the app (MIT License).
