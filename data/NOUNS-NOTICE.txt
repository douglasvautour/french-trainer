Nouns: nouns-data.js and noun-defs.js

1. Which nouns, and how frequent they are
   Source: Lexique 3.83, http://www.lexique.org — New, Pallier et al.
   ("Lexique 2: A New French Lexical Database", Behavior Research Methods 36(3), 2004).
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0),
            https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
   Used: the lemma frequencies of nouns (films and books), to rank the nouns and make the
         Common (1–500), Frequent (501–2,000) and Less Common (2,001–5,000) groups.

2. Gender, plurals, feminine forms, meanings and examples
   Source: the English Wiktionary, https://en.wiktionary.org, by Wiktionary contributors,
           extracted with Wiktextract (https://kaikki.org, Tatu Ylonen, LREC 2022).
   Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made for this app: only nouns among the 5,000 most frequent are kept; for the most frequent
nouns, the short English meanings, which nouns take du/de la, and a few feminine forms were corrected
by hand (tools/noun_fixes.txt); usage labels come from each sense's qualifiers; at most three short
example sentences are kept per sense. The wording of the meanings and examples on the Meanings
screens is unchanged.
